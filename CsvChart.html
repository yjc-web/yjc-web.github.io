CsvChart Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

Summary: CsvChart (including the CsvChart Pro lifetime add-on) processes all CSV, TSV, and log files, waveforms, tables, and exported reports 100% locally on your PC. We never collect, upload, or transmit your files or personal data to any external server.

1. 100% Local Data Processing & Storage

  • Local File Analysis & Exporting: Opening single or multi-day CSV/TSV/LOG files, multi-channel waveform rendering, dual-cursor measurements, peak searching, data table filtering, and exporting PNG images or Excel (.xlsx) reports happen strictly offline inside your computer's memory and local storage. Your data never leaves your device.
  • Local Preferences: UI preferences (language, dark/light theme, last opened folder path, channel favorite presets, and local trial/license state) are stored exclusively on your PC under %LOCALAPPDATA%\FlowSuiteYJC.CsvChart and can be deleted at any time or by uninstalling the App.

2. Microsoft Store Purchases & License Verification

  • Microsoft Store & In-App Purchases (CsvChart Pro): App downloads, Durable add-on purchases, billing, and entitlement verification are handled directly by Microsoft via official Windows.Services.Store APIs. FlowSuiteYJC never receives, processes, or stores your payment details, credit card numbers, or Microsoft account credentials.
  • Standalone License Key Activation (Optional): Only if you manually enter an external standalone license key, an anonymized hardware identifier hash (HWID) and device name are sent solely to our license verification endpoint to enforce activation limits. Offline enterprise keys are verified locally.

3. No Third-Party Sharing, Ads, or Tracking

We do not sell, rent, or share any user information. CsvChart contains zero advertising SDKs, third-party analytics trackers, or background telemetry.

4. Children's Privacy

CsvChart is a technical productivity and engineering visualization tool and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if app features or legal requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
